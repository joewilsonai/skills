## Edit decisions

Use one row per actual attempt. Link the trial record or the reviewed revisions. Record any useful evidence the simpler edit drops. Distinguish a winning excerpt from a verified delivery.

For a playback test, include the viewer’s unaided answer, whether the follow-up or a replay changed it, and any specific detail still missing. Identify human or model, prior familiarity, viewing order, sound and display conditions. Use “unmeasured” where no first-view evidence exists.
